Import order recommendation

  1. Programmes
  2. Student Groups
  3. Programme Courses
  4. Venues
  5. Master Timetable (creates Sessions)
  6. Workshop Allocation
  7. TD Allocation

The workshop & TD allocations preserve the exact venue text from the file (e.g. TW101) with no foreign key, as specified in Phase 1. The Workshop Allocation importer also accepts the raw university Workshop Schedule workbook directly — it auto-detects the matrix layout and extracts week ranges, workshop, position and Morning/Afternoon periods from the workbook. The Master Timetable importer asks you to pick the target academic semester on the upload form (it is never guessed or auto-created) and automatically links every LECTURE session to the programme groups that study the course, so full lecture cohorts are ready right after import. The report panel lists everything that was created or skipped so you always know what happened.
